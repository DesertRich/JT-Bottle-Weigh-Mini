Inventory Mathematics

Primary: liquid grams = (gross − tare) × 28.349523125; liquid mL = liquid grams ÷ density; liquid oz = mL ÷ 29.5735295625; bottle fraction = mL ÷ bottle size mL. Fallback: (gross − tare) ÷ (full − tare). Results are clamped to 0–100% of nominal bottle volume.
